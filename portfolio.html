About

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering student at Northern University Bangladesh, working across AI/ML, systems programming, and applied research. My coursework spans Java object-oriented design, machine learning fundamentals, and computer architecture — and outside of class, I write research papers on how privacy and security actually hold up (or don't) in widely used consumer platforms in Bangladesh.

My work tends to sit at the intersection of building and studying: implementing a working system, then asking what could go wrong with it for real users.
